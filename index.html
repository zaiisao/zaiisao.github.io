Selected Research

Extending LMs to Low-Resource Languages
Extending a multilingual language model to support low-resource languages with unseen scripts (Coptic, Syriac, Gothic, OCS Glagolitic, Samaritan). Results yield 8–106× perplexity reduction over conventional fine-tuning. Paper in preparation.
VBPM (Variational Bar Pointer Model)
Replaces the traditional Dynamic Bayesian Network in beat tracking pipelines with a fully differentiable variational model over structured latent variables, allowing for end-to-end training.
KineGuard
Skeleton-based content safety classifier for real-time unsafe motion detection. Built multi-GPU video processing pipeline with WHAM inference.
